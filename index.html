Block's theorem in Lean 4

Cohesive modules over the Dolbeault algebra of a compact complex manifold and perfect complexes of holomorphic sheaves: Ho(P_A) ≃ D_perf(X, O_X).

Everything is proved: there is no sorry, and the three main theorems depend only on the axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. Built on Lean 4 with Mathlib v4.35.0.

Main results

Let X be a compact Hausdorff complex manifold modelled on a finite-dimensional complex space E, A = 𝒜_X(X) its Dolbeault algebra of smooth (0, q)-forms with ∂̄, and O_X the sheaf of holomorphic functions.

  • Block's theorem (MainStatement, proved as main_theorem). The functor J : Ho(P_A) ⥤ D(O_X), M ↦ α(M), is full and faithful with essential image D_perf(X, O_X). [Block, Thm 4.1.3], in the perfect form of [Antweiler, Thm 7.2.20].
  • Conditional form (MainStatementConditional, proved as main_theorem_conditional). The same statement assuming the Dolbeault–Grothendieck lemma (H1) and the local gauge lemma (H2). Both are now theorems, so this is a corollary.
  • Twisted theorem (MainStatementTwisted, proved as main_theorem_twisted). For a ∂̄-closed h ∈ 𝒜^{0,2}(X) with a twist datum, cohesive modules over the curved algebra (𝒜_X(X), ∂̄, h) are equivalent to twisted perfect complexes. [Antweiler, Thm 7.4.3].
