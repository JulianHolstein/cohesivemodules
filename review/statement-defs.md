A cochain complex `K` of sheaves of `R.over V`-modules (sheaves on the open `V`) is perfect if every point `x ∈ V` has an open neighbourhood `W ≤ V` with complexes `L`, `P` of `R.over W`-modules, `P` strictly perfect, and quasi-isomorphisms `K|W ← L → P` (restriction `restrOver R hW` applied termwise). This is S9.b (`IsPerfect`) with `restrOver` in place of `restr`; for `V = ⊤` and `K = K'|⊤` it is equivalent to `IsPerfect K'` (`isPerfectOver_restr_top_iff`). ⚠ Roof of quasi-isomorphisms instead of an isomorphism in `D(R|W)`; equivalent.
- [`Cohesive.TwIsPerfect`](../Cohesivemodules/Sheaf/Twisted/PerfectDef.lean#L57) — def. **Z3.j. Twisted perfect complexes.** [A, Def 7.2.11], [Căldăraru, §2], blueprint-tw D12. A cochain complex `K` of `ȟ`-twisted sheaves is perfect if, for every `α`, its component complex `K_α = comp_α(K)` (a complex of sheaves of `O|U_α`-modules on `Over U_α`) is perfect (`IsPerfectOver`). ⚠ Componentwise definition; the gluing maps play no role.
- [`Cohesive.TwPerf`](../Cohesivemodules/Sheaf/Twisted/PerfectDef.lean#L66) — def. **Z3.j. `D_perf(X, ȟ)`.** The full subcategory (as an `ObjectProperty`) of the derived category `D(X, ȟ) = DerivedCategory (TwMod R U ch)` on the objects isomorphic to `Q K` for some twisted perfect complex `K`; the twisted analogue of `Perf` (S9.c). Closed under isomorphism by construction. The derived category is taken with respect to the abelian structure of Z3.h.
